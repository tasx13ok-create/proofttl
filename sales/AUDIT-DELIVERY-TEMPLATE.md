# Fact Audit delivery record

Customer / intake ID: ___
Accepted scope date: ___ | Verified payment date: ___
Human reviewer: ___ | Delivery deadline: ___
Customer-approved delivery location: ___

List each output, exact atomic claims, consequence rank, deep-check selection and exclusions. Never silently reduce scope.

For every finding record:
- ID and exact claim, preserving qualifiers/timeframe.
- Source URL, publisher, access time, relevant short excerpt and snapshot/hash where permitted.
- Supporting evidence, challenging evidence, and missing evidence. Absence alone is not contradiction.
- SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / UNKNOWN with scoped reasoning.
- Uncertainty, inaccessible sources, alternative interpretations and freshness limits.
- Suggested repair, separate from the original.
- Named human approval and timestamp.
- Fact Lease ID/signature only if actually issued and verified; otherwise “not issued.”

Summarize scoped/deeply checked counts, material findings and unresolved items. Do not imply legal/regulatory assurance.

Record verified Stripe state, report SHA-256, approved HTTPS delivery URL, customer access check and delivery time. Keep private material out of public Git.

Watch log: day 0 baseline, days 1–6 checks, day 7 final reread. Record source changes, actions, notices and actual lease-monitoring coverage. A database watch end time does not prove monitoring happened.
