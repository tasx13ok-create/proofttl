# ProofTTL Fact Audit

## Offer

**$1,500 paid upfront. One fixed scope. No tiers.**

The client sends up to 25 real AI-generated outputs their users actually see. ProofTTL performs an adversarial factual audit and returns a decision-ready evidence packet.

## Deliverable

- Review of up to 25 submitted AI outputs.
- Deep verification of the highest-risk claims within those outputs.
- Verdict per material claim: supported, contradicted, stale, unverifiable, or materially incomplete.
- Primary/authoritative evidence attached to each material finding.
- Plain-English reason chain showing exactly why the claim holds or fails.
- Findings ranked by consequence if wrong, not by trivia value.
- Recommended correction or safer answer behavior for each material failure.
- Permanent proof page for each material finding.
- One consolidated audit export suitable for sharing internally.
- Seven days of monitoring on the highest-risk findings, followed by one final re-read.

## Scope guard

“Up to 25 outputs” does not promise identical research depth for every sentence. ProofTTL triages the set and performs deeper verification on the claims with the highest consequence, ambiguity, or evidence burden.

## Turnaround

Initial findings target: 72 hours after receiving the claim set.
Final audit packet: within 5 business days for normal scope.
Monitoring window: seven calendar days after delivery.

## Buyer

Built for founder-led or small teams shipping AI-generated answers where a wrong factual statement is expensive or visible: legal and compliance tools, financial/research products, healthcare administration, insurance/underwriting workflows, and agencies delivering AI output to clients.

## What this is not

- Not a generic “AI safety assessment.”
- Not penetration testing or a security review.
- Not legal, medical, or financial advice.
- Not a benchmark based on synthetic trivia.
- Not a promise that every possible error in a product will be found.

It is a focused adversarial audit of real outputs against authoritative evidence.

## Sales rule

Do not lead with this page cold. First show one concrete failure from the prospect's own product. The offer is the next step after they see the evidence.

## Checkout

Payment should be collected upfront through a single payment link. Do not offer invoice terms, net-30, procurement onboarding, or custom enterprise paperwork during the 9-day close window.
