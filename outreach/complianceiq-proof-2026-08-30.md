# ProofTTL Finding — ComplianceIQ

## A deadline ComplianceIQ says is already enforceable was moved to 2027/2028

**Target:** ComplianceIQ EU AI Act checklist / homepage

**Published claim**

ComplianceIQ's public checklist gives **August 2, 2026** as the due date for high-risk AI human-oversight requirements, and its FAQ says: **"The August 2, 2026 deadline is when enforcement begins for high-risk AI obligations."**

**Verdict:** CONTRADICTED / STALE DEADLINE

**Current primary law**

The consolidated EU AI Act, Article 113(c), as amended through July 27, 2026, phases the main high-risk Chapter III obligations to:

- **December 2, 2027** for Article 6(2) / Annex III high-risk systems; and
- **August 2, 2028** for Article 6(1) / Annex I high-risk product systems.

Primary source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/2026-07-27/eng

European Commission implementation page: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai

**Why it matters**

ComplianceIQ sells deadline tracking, regulatory classification and generated compliance documents. A stale high-risk deadline can cause a customer to be told an obligation is already overdue when the current law gives another 16–24 months.

**Recommended fix**

Separate the timeline by obligation. Keep Article 50 transparency duties at August 2, 2026 where applicable, but update Annex III high-risk obligations to December 2, 2027 and Annex I high-risk product obligations to August 2, 2028.

---

### Additional audit signal held in reserve

The same public homepage labels **"Does your AI tool process customer data?"** as **"High-risk classification."** The AI Act does not classify systems as high-risk merely because they process customer data; Article 6 ties classification to specified Annex I/III conditions and use cases.
