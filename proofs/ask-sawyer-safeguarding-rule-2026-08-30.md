# Fact Proof — Ask Sawyer / SEC Safeguarding Rule

**Verdict: CONTRADICTED / STALE**

## Claim

Ask Sawyer's federal Advisor Agreement Law page states that the SEC's 2023 Safeguarding Rule proposal, Release IA-6240 / File S7-04-23, “remains proposed and has not been finalized.”

The page is marked **Last reviewed: 2026-07-13**.

Target:
https://asksawyer.ai/law/federal/documents/advisor-agreement/rules/

## Evidence

The SEC formally withdrew the Safeguarding Advisory Client Assets proposal in June 2025.

Primary source:
https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2025/06/safeguarding-advisory-client-assets

The SEC's rulemaking record identifies S7-04-23 / IA-6240 and states that the Commission does not intend to issue a final rule from the withdrawn proposal. The withdrawal became effective **2025-06-17**.

Rulemaking record:
https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/rulemaking-activity?search=S7-04-23

## Why the claim fails

“Not finalized” is technically weaker than the actual status. By Sawyer's stated July 2026 review date, the proposal had been **withdrawn for more than a year**. A withdrawn proposal is not a live pending proposal.

## Consequence

A user relying on the page for an investment-adviser agreement could treat abandoned proposed requirements as an active regulatory development. That can distort compliance planning, contract review, and risk prioritization.

## Recommended fix

Replace the pending-proposal language with a clear statement that IA-6240 / S7-04-23 was withdrawn effective June 17, 2025. Keep the currently effective Custody Rule separate from the abandoned Safeguarding proposal.

## Evidence boundary

This proof establishes a specific stale regulatory-status error on one public Sawyer page. It does **not** establish that every Sawyer answer is inaccurate, and it should not be represented that way.
